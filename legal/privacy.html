Privacy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

Claim the Curb is a shared street map for youth bottle drives. An adult runs the crew. The map is not a children's app and it does not ask for a child's name, age, school, or photo.

What the map stores

Street names that a crew marked as collected or held, the date, a team name an adult typed, and sometimes a bag count. That is so the next crew can skip those doors.

Location

The map can ask for your location only when you choose Share location, to check that you were on that block. You can say no. The map still works. We do not follow the phone in the background.

What we do not do

We do not sell names, emails, or locations. We do not put children's names on the map. We do not take payment-card numbers on this page.

Email

If you write to us, we keep your name, email, city, and the message so we can reply. Ask us to delete it and we will.

Alberta and Canada

We treat this as personal information under PIPEDA and Alberta's PIPA.
